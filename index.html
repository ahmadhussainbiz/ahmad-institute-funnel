What counts as a qualified enquiry?

The proposed starting definition is a unique, contactable prospective student interested in the agreed course, meeting its relevant location and entry requirements, who has reviewed tuition and schedule and is considering the agreed intake. I agree the final definition and confirmation method with you before launch. A form submission alone does not establish qualification. Duplicates, bots, job applicants and existing active enquiries are excluded. Tuition awareness is not financing approval.

How do the 15-day target and 30-day service relate?

The enquiry target is measured over the first 15 live advertising days after campaign launch. Setup happens beforehand. The management fee covers 30 live advertising days. The pilot does not guarantee paid enrolments, profit or recovery of advertising spend.

Who handles admissions, and what if setup fails?

Your nominated admissions contact handles student conversations, eligibility decisions and closing. My service supports routing, follow-up and tracking. I agree the setup deadline with you before onboarding; if an agency-caused setup failure prevents launch by that deadline, the setup fee and any unearned management fee are refunded. Measurement, interruption and refund procedures are agreed before launch.
