Before you book

What counts as a qualified enquiry?

We agree qualification and its confirmation method before launch: a unique, contactable prospective student interested in the agreed course and intake, meeting relevant location and entry requirements, who has reviewed tuition and schedule. A form alone is insufficient. Duplicates, bots, job applicants and existing active enquiries are excluded. Tuition awareness is not financing approval.

Are enquiries the same as paid enrolments?

No. Qualified enquiries are not paid enrolments, applications or booked admissions meetings. Enrolments are tracked separately; profit and recovery of advertising spend are not guaranteed.

Do you work with one course or several?

The service covers one agreed course and intake. Additional courses are scoped separately before launch.

Who contacts interested students?

Your nominated admissions contact handles conversations, eligibility decisions and closing. I support routing, consent-based email follow-up, consultation reminders and tracking.

How do the 15-day target and 30-day management period relate?

The target covers the first 15 live advertising days after campaign launch, following setup. Management covers 30 live advertising days. Neither window starts with payment.

What does the service include?

Advertising strategy, course qualification, admissions routing, follow-up support and reporting. Management covers 30 live advertising days. We agree scope with you before launch.

What happens if the enquiry target is missed?

Under the agreed conditions, missing the 30-qualified-enquiry target in 15 live advertising days triggers a management-fee refund. Completed setup, actual advertising spend and software remain payable; no renewal is required. Measurement, interruption and refund procedures are agreed before launch.

What if a setup failure prevents launch?

We agree the setup deadline before onboarding. If an agency-caused failure prevents launch by then, the setup fee and any unearned management fee are refunded. This is separate from the enquiry-target remedy.
