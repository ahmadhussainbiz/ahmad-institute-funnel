When interest doesn’t become enrolment

Your next intake is approaching.
Are the right students moving towards it?

An enquiry is a start. But when students stop replying after hearing the fee, or follow-up is missed, places can remain unfilled.

  1. They ask for details. Then stop replying.

    • You share the fees and course information.
    • The conversation ends before a clear next step.

  2. Follow-up takes time. Readiness stays unclear.

    • Your team keeps chasing interested people.
    • It is difficult to tell who is ready to join.

  3. The start date gets closer. Places stay open.

    • You have an upcoming intake and available seats.
    • You are still trying to reach suitable students.

  4. A valuable course still needs a clearer path.

    • Students need to understand why the course suits them.
    • First interest needs a clear route to an enrolment decision.

I connect your advertising, student qualification and admissions follow-up so suitable applicants have a clear next step towards enrolment.

The approach

How I help turn course interest into paid enrolments.

I connect the message students see, the information they share and the follow-up they receive. Your admissions team handles course decisions and enrolment conversations. I use that feedback to improve the advertising and follow-up.

  1. 01

    Start with your upcoming intake.

    I review your courses, fees, available seats and local competitors to agree which program to promote first.

  2. 02

    Give students a reason to choose you.

    I create ads around your instructors, practical learning and student stories using evidence your institute can support.

  3. 03

    Reach prospective students.

    I build campaigns around the selected course, teaching format and the locations you serve.

  4. 04

    Qualify interest before admissions follows up.

    I ask about course preferences, entry requirements, awareness of fees and intended start dates.

  5. 05

    Make the next step clear.

    I connect enquiries with your admissions team and set up agreed follow-up messages and reminders.

  6. 06

    Track progress through to paid enrolment.

    I review enquiry quality, admissions progress and advertising costs, then adjust the campaign using the outcomes.

Why this approach

Before the admissions conversation

Illustrative qualification workflow · not client results

  1. Which course and upcoming intake are you considering?
  2. Do you meet the course’s location and entry requirements?
  3. Have you reviewed the tuition and timetable?
  4. What would you like admissions to clarify before you decide?

Admissions handoff: course and intake, relevant answers, an agreed next step and a named follow-up owner. A submitted form alone does not establish qualification.

Pilot fit

Who it’s not for.

This pilot needs an agreed budget, an upcoming intake and an admissions team ready to follow up.

  • No agreed advertising budget

    You cannot commit to the advertising budget agreed before launch.

  • No upcoming intake or available seats

    You do not have a course starting or space for more students.

  • No admissions follow-up

    Nobody at the institute can respond to enquiries and handle enrolment conversations.

  • Overnight expectations

    You expect immediate enrolments without giving the campaign and admissions process time to work.

Desired outcome

What success can look like.

Your upcoming classes are filling with paid students. Your admissions team is speaking with suitable applicants who understand the fees and intended start date. You have better visibility into what brings enrolments, helping you plan your next intake, instructors and revenue.

If the agreed target is missed.

The numbers need to work for you.

Before launch, we look at your course price, payment timing and the cost of teaching each additional student. The aim is for new enrolments to cover advertising and management costs while leaving a worthwhile margin. Revenue alone does not establish profit.

Before you book

What counts as a paid enrolment?

A new student attributed to the agreed campaign, accepted into the selected program, who has paid the agreed course fee or qualifying deposit. Attribution, payment and refund treatment are defined in the written terms agreed before launch. An enquiry or submitted form alone is not a paid enrolment.

When do the 15 live advertising days start?

The target counts the days your campaign actually runs after launch. Setup happens before that period begins.

Who handles admissions conversations?

Your nominated admissions contact handles conversations, eligibility decisions and enrolment. I support routing, consent-based email follow-up, consultation reminders and tracking.

Can the pilot cover several courses?

The pilot covers one agreed program and intake. Additional courses are scoped separately before launch.

What happens if the target is missed?

See My guarantee in the offer above. Advertising spend paid directly to Meta, completed setup and software charges remain payable. Measurement, interruption and refund procedures are agreed before launch.

What costs are separate?

Setup, advertising and software are separate from the management fee. The advertising budget is agreed separately and paid directly to Meta. Management covers 30 live advertising days; the target covers 15 live advertising days. Scope, fees and budget coverage are agreed before launch. No renewal is required.

What happens on the 20-minute call?

We review one course, its fees, available places and intake timing, then discuss the admissions process and whether the pilot is a sensible fit. No purchase or immediate decision is required to book.

What if a setup failure prevents launch?

We agree the setup deadline before onboarding. If an agency-caused failure prevents launch by then, the setup fee and any unearned management fee are refunded. This is separate from the paid-enrolment target remedy.
