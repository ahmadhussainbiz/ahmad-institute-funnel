# Internal change summary and commercial decisions

23 October 2026. Review branch: `review/funnel-design-refinements`.

## Change in the review draft

The attached revision brief replaces the former target of 30 unique qualified course enquiries with **30 paid enrolments within 15 live advertising days** for one assessed and agreed program. This is a proposed offer, not evidence of past performance. Enquiries remain an earlier stage in the process.

The draft uses the requested management-fee refund statement once under “My guarantee”. It explains that advertising spend paid to Meta remains an institute cost and preserves the existing separate setup, advertising and software disclosures. No numeric public prices are restored; the owner removed them on 20 October.

The draft removes “zero financial risk”. It does not publish the proposed payment trigger, unlimited free work, a 50-plus monthly result claim or unspecified commercial terms. The preferred closing guidance is to reply to the message that brought the visitor to the page, with the existing Calendly link retained as an optional direct route.

## Owner decisions before publication

1. **Management fee and payment trigger.** Reconcile the management fee and collection timing with the exact proposed term: “Management payment begins after the first 15 paid enrolments”. Explain how a full management-fee refund operates if the target is missed, including when no fee has yet been collected. This proposed trigger remains internal until agreed.

2. **Counting paid enrolments.** Agree the selected program and intake, eligibility, qualifying course fee or deposit, campaign attribution method and window, evidence of acceptance/payment, duplicate or existing-student treatment, and how withdrawals, cancellations, student refunds or reversed payments affect the count. No such rules or attribution window are invented in the draft.

3. **Target measurement and refund procedure.** Agree how live advertising days are counted and verified, how campaign interruptions or pauses are handled, the respective institute/agency responsibilities, eligibility conditions, and the management-fee refund process and deadline. Written terms are still required; the page does not establish that an executed attachment exists.

4. **Expenses and existing remedies.** Reconcile the revised offer with the previously confirmed treatment: completed setup, actual advertising spend and software charges remain payable; no renewal is required. Advertising budget is agreed separately and paid directly to Meta. Confirm software/tool costs and coverage periods and whether the separate agency-caused setup-failure remedy remains applicable. Do not imply that all costs are refundable or that the institute has zero financial risk.

5. **Service duration and setup.** The saved implementation states a management period of 30 live advertising days, distinct from the 15-live-day target. Confirm whether that duration continues under the paid-enrolment offer rather than silently replacing it. Confirm setup scope and deadline, advertising-budget coverage and software periods; no replacement periods or deadline are invented.

6. **Ongoing free work.** Decide whether this is offered alongside or instead of a refund, and define scope, duration, limits, responsibilities and expense treatment. Keep the promise out of public copy until it is reconciled with the payment and refund model.

7. **50-plus monthly enrolments after scaling.** Supply evidence and define the circumstances, limits and meaning of the claim before considering publication. It remains omitted; no monthly outcome or scaling result is promised.

Show the agreed eligibility and expense treatment plainly before commercial launch. Keep the review version unmerged, undeployed and unindexed while these decisions remain open.
